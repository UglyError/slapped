# Slapped Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

This policy explains what Slapped collects and why. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

## What we collect
| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address and password (stored hashed) | To create and secure your account |
| Username, display name, bio, avatar | Shown on your public profile |
| Photos of stickers you scan | Shown publicly as your finds |
| Precise location **of each sticker you log** | Shown publicly on the map and the sticker page; used to stop duplicates and show nearby stickers |
| Your current location while using Explore | To list stickers near you. It's sent only for that search and not stored |
| Props, comments, follows, trades, blocks and reports | To provide the social and trading features |

Location is only used while the app is open ("When In Use"). Photos you pick or take are processed on your device to cut out the sticker. Only the finished sticker image is uploaded.

## What's public
Your profile, the stickers you've found or own, where they were found, your comments and your trade history on a sticker are visible to other users. Trade offers and blocks are private.

## Where it's stored
Data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which processes it on our behalf.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete it. **Profile → Settings → Delete account** permanently deletes your account, profile, stickers, images, comments, props, follows and trades. Reports you filed may be kept to enforce our Terms.

## Children
Slapped isn't for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Your rights
You can view and edit your profile in the app, and delete everything with the delete-account option. For any other request, contact us below.
